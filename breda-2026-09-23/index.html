Wednesday, hour by hour.

This is the organisers' own timetable for the Ecosystem Day, the one in the workshop pack, not a guess of mine. Five rooms run the same clock in parallel and you are in room 3, Sovereignty.

Workshop 3 · Sovereignty · your room The question the room answers is how a city organises sovereignty and democracy over its AI layer. Your three work items, in the organisers' words: a European definition of AI in the city, mandatory public registers of the urban AI systems, and a right to explanation when an automated decision touches a resident. You open the session with the short introduction.
Own laptop, the stage test is on it USB-C to HDMI adapter Clicker Slides went to the organisers on 20 September Tomorrow: at the Chassé by 11:05
  • 13:00
    Introduction. The chair opens with the short film by Citney and sets the core question, then everyone says their name, their role and one sentence on why this theme is urgent for them. Your short introduction goes here. The conversation is recorded on Dembrane for the report.
  • 13:30
    Input from the international participants. Short presentations from the people who came for this theme. Nothing of yours on the clock, this is the block to listen in and note who is in the room.
  • 14:00
    The end vision for 2036. Everyone writes their own thoughts first and ranks them on the BlackMirror to Her scale, the middle comes out, and the room keeps one utopian or dystopian vision in a hundred words. The pack says this is the block that decides the day, so it is the one to hold tight if the room drifts.
  • 14:30
    Break.
  • 14:45
    The vision as a film. The group picks a protagonist and writes five frames, starting from the last one, then uploads the script to the film app and the clip is generated. The clearest thing on this page to show a room that is arguing about abstraction.
  • 15:15
    Backcasting. One timeline per table, filled backwards from 2036. What stands in 2035 and who steers it, which agreements are running in 2030, what started or was tested in 2027, and what has to begin before the end of 2026 with a name against it.
  • 15:45
    Three recommendations. The group writes its common position as three concrete recommendations, addressed to the EU, to national governments or to cities among themselves. They are folded into the Breda Declaration on Local Tech.
  • 16:15
    The editorial group of five, one per workshop, works the day's contributions into the Declaration from the transcripts. If your room sent you, this is where the next hour goes. I did not read who sits on it, so I am not naming anyone.
  • 16:30
    Plenary feedback. The five groups present to each other, 45 minutes for the whole set. The Declaration can be signed on the spot and it is presented at the congress tomorrow.
  • 17:00
    Networking borrel. The day ends with a drink at the hotel, until six. This is the hour where the consortium conversations actually happen, and the walk into the centre is nine minutes from the door.

What I would do after five.

Five things from the city, each with the reason it is on the page. Nothing here is booked and the page never books anything on its own.

Tonight · Havermarkt · from 20:00

De Silent Muziekbingo

The municipality's own event calendar has it granted for 23 September on the Havermarkt, and it runs every Wednesday from May to the end of the season. You buy a ticket, take a seat on the terrace of the Van Asch cafés, put the headphones on and cross songs off a card instead of numbers, with a show around it. Headphones and card are in the price.

It is the one thing tonight that is unmistakably this city on a Wednesday and not a conference spillover, and the Havermarkt is five minutes from your hotel door. Tickets go through their own shop and the site says it is usually full. The Havermarkt edition.

Shall I open the ticket page on your phone so you can decide after the borrel?

Today and every day · 17:00 to 22:00

The light artwork at 't Zoet

An interactive light show inside an old sugar silo on the site of the former CSM factory, designed by a Breda lighting studio with students from Avans. Avans is the partner you spent yesterday and today next to in the DETECT 2.0 meetings, so this is the same city showing what its own students build when the brief is not a slide deck.

It is 1.7 km from the hotel, twenty-three minutes on foot, and it runs until ten at night, which makes it the natural end of the evening rather than the start. The listing.

Shall I put it after the bingo, or do you want the evening to stay in the centre?

Tonight · Café Lievense · 20:00

The Wednesday jam

Café Lievense and a house band run an open jam every Wednesday, instruments are there, and you can also just sing a number karaoke-style. Entry is free and it is five minutes from the hotel on the Prinsenkade.

I will be honest about the match: your own shelf is Glass, Mertens, Pärt and Richter, and a jam session is not that. What it is, is a room full of people playing live instruments on a Wednesday, free, and closer to your door than the hotel bar. The listing.

Shall I keep it as the plan B if the Havermarkt is sold out?

Until 25 October · across the centre

BredaPhoto, with the city as the gallery

The photo festival runs from 11 September to 25 October in indoor and outdoor locations around the centre, the Chassé park among them, and the station has a selection of young talent up until the end of October. Photography on real walls in a real city, which is the part of your taste that no congress session covers.

I read the city listings and the festival name, not their own programme, so treat the hours as theirs to confirm. BredaPhoto.

Shall I read the programme and pick the three locations closest to the hotel?

Only if the day ends early · de Haven

The boat through the singels

The electric boats of De Nieuwe Mark leave from the Haven every day, and in September the last departure is at 17:00, which collides with the borrel and with the plenary that comes before it. I am putting it here so you know it exists, not so you run for it. The skipper talks you past the Spanjaardsgat, the castle and the Koepel.

Shall I look at tomorrow's sailings instead, for after your keynote?
