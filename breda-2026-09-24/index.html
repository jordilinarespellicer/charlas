What the city offers you.

Four things I matched to you, each with the reason. No generic top ten, and every one of them is a link you can check.

Image · 400 m from your stage

BredaPhoto Festival 2026

Twelfth edition, 11 September to 25 October, more than forty photographers in churches, parks and a former women's prison. The festival hub is Carré Chassé, which is the building you are speaking in.

It matches you because photography and image are half of your work, and because you will be standing inside the hub anyway. The city agenda lists 10:00 to 17:00 daily. The outdoor work in the Koepeldistrict and at XXL Square is the part you can walk past without a ticket.

bredaphoto.nl

Street art · a walk

Blind Walls Gallery

The painted walls of Breda, spread over the city centre as an open air gallery, with a guided walking tour listed by the city's visitor platform.

It matches you because murals are image and storytelling at the scale of a street, and because the way to see them is to walk. One hour, no booking, no bench.

blindwalls.gallery · the tour dates

Music · 508 m from the venue

The Grote Kerk and its organ

A fourteenth century church with the Vierdag organ inside and a concert series, BACH & CO, built around it. The series runs on Sundays in July and August, so it is not on during your week, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

It still matches you: organ and sacred music sit next to Pärt in your listening, and the building is worth the ten minute walk on its own.

grotekerkbreda.nl
